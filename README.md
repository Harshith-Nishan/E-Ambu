**E Ambu – Automated Resuscitator**

Developed an automated resuscitator designed to provide emergency respiratory support by automating the manual operation of an Ambu bag. The system integrates embedded electronics, sensors, and mechanical actuation to deliver controlled ventilation during critical situations. The project was focused on creating an affordable, portable, and easy-to-deploy solution for emergency and healthcare environments.

Through this project, I worked on product design, embedded systems development, sensor integration, prototyping, testing, validation, and healthcare technology innovation while addressing a real-world medical challenge.
